# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be precise, respectful, and constructive in issues and reviews.
- Critique code, architecture, evidence, and risk rather than people.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and security-sensitive context.
- Do not publish credentials, private logs, user data, or exploit details in a
  public issue.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information,
and disruption of project collaboration are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect contributors and users.
